dsh-lan-guard `0.4.0` · 已验证 DeepSeek Harness `0.1.7-rc.2`

<h3 id="cn-v0.4.0">🐛 修复 — 手机端「会话载入不全，甚至断开」</h3>

会话记录只走 WebSocket（`/api/remote.mux` 上的 `session/follow`），会话列表、目标条、统计数字则来自 HTTP——所以症状的形状是「界面都在、对话区永久停在『载入历史…』」。

三处实测把这个形状解释清楚：

- 宿主每 2 秒 Ping 一次，连续两次没被回应就断开（`websocketHeartbeatIntervalMs: 2000`、`MAX_MISSED_HEARTBEATS: 2`）。
- 对真实 DSH 实测：停止回 Pong 后 **6.0 秒**被切断（`close 1006`，连 Close 帧都没有），三处独立复现。
- 手机锁屏/切走时页面被系统挂起，回不了心跳；WebKit 另有「后台恢复后 `new WebSocket()` 永久卡 CONNECTING 而 HTTP 正常」的已知问题。

<h3>✨ 新增 — 三层兜底，默认全开、可逐项关闭</h3>

- **代理代答心跳（`answerHeartbeat`）**：代理在中转时读取上游→浏览器方向的 WS 控制帧，遇到 Ping 立刻以客户端身份回一个掩码 Pong。**实测：停回心跳 6 秒被切断 → 代答后 20 秒以上存活。** 转发仍是纯字节管道，观察器最多只累积 14 字节头，异常即退出。
- **断线看门狗（`socketWatchdog`）**：卡在 CONNECTING 超过 8 秒的 socket 会被关掉；从后台回来 10 秒后仍一条都没连上时重载一次（每标签最多连续 3 次，冷却 20 秒起）。
- **移动端兼容垫片（`mobileCompat`）**：补 `AbortSignal.any`/`AbortSignal.timeout`/`Promise.withResolvers`/`Iterator` 与移动端 meta。缺这些 API 时 DSH 客户端在会话流里抛错，界面只会一直显示「载入历史…」且没有报错。**不禁用页面缩放。**

<h3 id="cn-v0.4.0-doctor">🩺 新增 — 连接体检</h3>

「设置 → 局域网访问 → 连接与证书 → 手机连接与自愈」里可以一键体检：缺哪些 API、页面拿到了哪些补丁、对当前地址做一次真实 WebSocket 握手，并显示代理侧计数（在活连接 / 累计升级 / 被拒 / 代答心跳次数）与最近一条连接的时长、上下行字节、是否异常断开。

<h3>✅ 验证</h3>

- 测试套件 **349 项**全绿（`0.3.6` 为 330）。
- 心跳代答有端到端用例：上游按 40ms 心跳、漏 2 次即销毁 —— 关掉代答必被回收、打开代答连接存活。
- 注入的页面脚本在测试里被执行（假环境 + 假时钟）：超时关 socket、有 OPEN 不重载、无 socket 不重载、后台恢复才重载一次并按冷却与上限退避、隐藏时不重载。
- 浏览器实测（Chrome 154，经临时回环转发走完整插件链路）：WS 握手 3ms；首帧 2.1MB 单帧 43–110ms；12 秒 310 帧无中断；无代答的连接在页面冻结 12 秒后收到 `close 1006`。

<h3 id="en-v0.4.0">🐛 Fixed — a phone's session loading incompletely, then disconnecting</h3>

The session transcript rides the WebSocket only (`session/follow` on `/api/remote.mux`); the session list, the goal chip and the statistics arrive over HTTP. That is why the failure looks like "everything is there except the conversation", stuck on 「载入历史…」.

Three measurements explain the shape:

- The host pings every 2 s and drops a socket after two unanswered pings (`websocketHeartbeatIntervalMs: 2000`, `MAX_MISSED_HEARTBEATS: 2`).
- Against the real DSH, a client that stops sending Pongs is dropped after **6.0 s** (`close 1006`, no Close frame) — reproduced three independent ways.
- A phone that locks its screen or switches away cannot answer; WebKit additionally has a case where `new WebSocket()` stays in CONNECTING forever after a resume while HTTP keeps working.

<h3>✨ Added — three layers, all on by default, each switchable</h3>

- **`answerHeartbeat`**: while relaying, the proxy reads the upstream→browser control frames and answers a Ping with a masked Pong on the client's behalf. **Measured: 6 s reap without it, 20 s+ survival with it.** The relay stays a byte-for-byte pipe; the observer accumulates at most a 14-byte header and disables itself on any anomaly.
- **`socketWatchdog`**: a socket stuck in CONNECTING for 8 s is closed, and a page resumed with nothing open reloads itself once (at most three in a row per tab, backing off from 20 s).
- **`mobileCompat`**: fills in `AbortSignal.any`/`AbortSignal.timeout`/`Promise.withResolvers`/`Iterator` and the mobile metas. Without those APIs DSH's client throws inside its session-stream path and the UI sits on 「载入历史…」 with no error at all. **Page zoom is not disabled.**

<h3 id="en-v0.4.0-doctor">🩺 Added — connection health check</h3>

Under **Settings → 局域网访问 → 连接与证书 → 手机连接与自愈**: which APIs this browser lacks, which patches the page received, a real WebSocket handshake against the current origin, plus the proxy-side counters (open connections, upgrades, refusals, answered heartbeats) and the last connection's duration, bytes and whether it died abnormally.

<h3>✅ Verification</h3>

- Suite green at **349** specs (330 in `0.3.6`).
- An end-to-end spec reproduces the heartbeat pair against a DSH-shaped upstream (40 ms pings, destroyed after two misses): reaps without the answerer, survives with it.
- The injected page scripts are executed in the suite (fake environment and clock): connect timeout closes a stuck socket, no reload while a socket is OPEN, no reload on a page that never created one, one reload after a resume with nothing open plus cooldown/cap backoff, never while hidden.
- Browser verification (Chrome 154, through a temporary loopback forward that still traverses the whole plugin path): 3 ms handshake, a 2.1 MB single-frame snapshot in 43–110 ms, 310 frames over 12 s with no interruption, and `close 1006` on the connection without the answerer after a 12 s main-thread freeze.
