dsh-lan-guard `0.4.4` · 已验证 DeepSeek Harness `0.2.0-rc.1`、`0.1.7-rc.2`

<h3 id="cn-v0.4.4">📱 修好 iOS 上「打开会话停不到最新消息、手指几乎拖不动」</h3>

DSH `0.2.0-rc.1` 下，官方滚动层**内部**有一层 `overflow:hidden` 且高度被锁死，把会话内容裁掉，高度传不上去 —— 真机实测 **21083px 的内容只换来 336px 的可滚范围**。所以打开会话不在最新消息，手指也几乎拖不动。

`mobileScrollFix` 现在补偿这一层：让它长高（`height:auto`）并脱离滚动容器身份（`overflow:visible`），可滚范围恢复到 **20675px**。改前自检、无效整体回退，页面保持与官方一致。

- 输入框始终吸底，官方右下角「回到底部」按钮回到原位（两项都是真机可测量的验收项）。
- 只动官方滚动层**内部**那一层：不新建滚动容器，不改官方其余 UI。

<h3>升级</h3>

```sh
dsh plugin --profile web add dsh-lan-guard@latest
```

重启一次 dsh 生效。

<h3 id="en-v0.4.4">📱 Fix iOS "session does not open at the newest message / barely drags"</h3>

On DSH `0.2.0-rc.1` a layer **inside** the official scroll layer is `overflow:hidden` with a locked height and clips the conversation, so its height never reaches the scroller — measured on device: **21083px of content behind a 336px scroll range**. A session therefore does not open at the newest message and barely drags.

`mobileScrollFix` now compensates that layer: grow it (`height:auto`) and drop its scroll-container role (`overflow:visible`), restoring the range to **20675px**. The change is self-checked and reverted wholesale when it does not widen the range.

- The composer stays pinned to the bottom and the official "back to bottom" button returns to its place (both measured on device).
- Only the layer *inside* the official scroller is touched: no extra scroll container, no restyling of the official UI.
